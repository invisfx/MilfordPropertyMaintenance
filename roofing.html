Protecting Your Home from the Top Down

Your roof is your home's first line of defense. Milford Property Maintenance provides professional roofing services including repairs, replacements, inspections, and storm damage restoration for residential properties throughout the Tri-State Area.

Frequently Asked Questions

What roofing materials do you work with?

We work with asphalt shingles, architectural shingles, metal roofing, and flat roofing systems. We'll recommend the best option for your home and budget.

Do you handle storm damage repairs?

Yes. We respond quickly to storm damage and can work with your insurance company to streamline the claims process.

How often should I have my roof inspected?

We recommend annual inspections, especially after severe weather. Regular inspections can catch small issues before they become costly repairs.
